Privacy Policy

Digital Lesela Sete

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Digital Lesela Sete (the app) handles personal data in the Android and web versions.

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, first and last name, nickname, birthdate, gender, and style preferences. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is not visible to us.
  • Profile photo, wardrobe photos, item details (category, colour, season, style tags), outfits, events, and feedback you give on outfit suggestions.

Information from sign-in providers

If you sign in with Google or Microsoft (or Apple, where offered), we receive your name and email address from that provider.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera and photo library: to add wardrobe and profile photos.
  • Calendar: only if you allow it, the app reads and writes events on your device calendar for event planning.
  • Notifications: to remind you about planned events.
  • The app does not read your device location.

Weather

Weather forecasts are requested from Open-Meteo using a default location. Your device location is not sent.

How we use information

  • To run the app: generate outfit suggestions, show wardrobe insights, keep your wardrobe in sync, and secure your account.
  • We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage) stores and processes account and app data.
  • Open-Meteo receives the default location coordinates used for forecasts.

Deleting your data

Profile > Delete Account permanently deletes your account, wardrobe photos, outfits, events, insight history, and profile photo.

You can also request deletion from the account deletion page.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under 13.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Firebase security rules limit each account to its own data.

Changes

We will update this policy and the in-app copy when our practices change.
